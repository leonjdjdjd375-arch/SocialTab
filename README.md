SocialMod – Your Social Minecraft Experience

SocialMod expands Minecraft with an extensive social, community, and hosting system. Connect with friends, share screenshots and gameplay clips, create private groups, and discover new servers – all directly within Minecraft, without constantly switching between different applications.

SocialMod combines social features, server management, and useful client-side features in one single mod.

Features

Friends & Social

Add and manage friends
Player profiles with custom avatars and banners
Follow other players and gain followers
Set your own status
Block or mute players
Activity feed showing your friends' activities
Notifications when friends start Minecraft
Notifications when friends join or leave a server
See when your friends are online
Join servers directly through player profiles
Invite friends to your own server
Manage join requests

Social Feed & Posts

Publish your own posts
Share Minecraft screenshots directly
Share short gameplay clips
Like and comment on posts
Use different reactions
Make posts visible only to friends
Show achievements on your profile

Screenshot Integration

After taking a screenshot with F2, a selection menu appears

Save – Save the screenshot to your Pictures folder
Delete – Delete the screenshot
Open – Open the screenshot
Publish – Share the screenshot directly in the Social Feed

Waypoint Integration

SocialMod provides server-based waypoint management
